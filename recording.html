Why these rules

A phone clip becomes a 3D game clip through SAM 3 (mask), then SAM 3D Body (pose), then clip-builder. SAM 3D Body reads one image at a time: it measures pose well and depth badly.

Travel ACROSS the frameAcross the frame: ~1–2 cm error at 5 m. Toward or away from the camera: ~5–15 cm per frame. On our step-back take, 1.6 m of depth travel had to be thrown away.
Camera never movesTripod, 1x lens, settings locked. SAM guesses the focal length on every frame, so a fixed lens keeps that guess stable.
Same size in frameWalking 3 m toward a camera 5 m away changes body size by ~60 %, and all of it reads as depth noise.
Hold still at the edges≥ 1 s in the idle stance at the start and end of every action. The floor snap and foot pinning lock onto those frames.

Values marked (est.) are research estimates. Tune them on real takes. The same guide is in docs/RECORDING.md.

Rules

Tap a rule to open it.

Cameratripod · 0.9–1.2 m · level · 1x · landscape · 60 fps · locked
  • Tripod, fully still. No pan, zoom or cuts. Keep one tripod position per session; if it moves, record a new calibration clip.
  • Lens height 0.9–1.2 m (hip to chest). Keep it level: pitch 0°, at most 5–10° down.
  • 1x main lens, landscape 16:9. No ultra-wide.
  • Stabilisation / Action Mode OFF and HDR OFF. Lock exposure, focus and white balance (for example with the Blackmagic Camera app).
  • 60 fps, 1080p or better. Use 4K60 for the sprint lane and for shots.
  • Shutter 1/500–1/1000 in daylight, or 1/120 under LEDs (1/100 on 50 Hz mains) to avoid flicker. Ball blur at 5 m/s (est.): 8 cm at 1/60, 4 cm at 1/120, 1 cm at 1/500.
  • Light behind the camera. Plain background with no mirrors or glass. Nobody else in view.
Distance3.5 m in place · 4.5 m shots · 5 m lanes · 6.5 m sprint

A 1x phone lens covers about 1.32·d wide × 0.745·d tall (est.). Tape start, centre and end marks parallel to the camera. At the ends of travel keep ≥ 1 m to the frame edge.

Framingwhole body + ball · 10 % under the feet · 0.8–1 m headroom
  • Whole body plus the ball in frame for the entire take.
  • ≥ 10 % margin under the feet: heels, toes and the ball's bounce must be visible.
  • Headroom ≥ 0.8–1.0 m above the standing head for shots and layups. Keep the ball in frame ≥ 0.3 s after release.
  • The performer fills ≥ half the frame height where possible.
Real failure: in one step-back frame the head left the top of the frame. SAM guessed the rest of the body and faked a 35 cm hop. The pipeline now drops such frames and interpolates them, but record with headroom.
Performerfitted clothes · contrasting shoes · size 7 ball · alone
  • Fitted shorts above the knee and a fitted top. Patterns or light colours read better than all-black.
  • Shoes that contrast with the floor. Use a matte floor; glossy courts reflect the legs.
  • Size 7 ball only: the pipeline assumes a 0.12 m radius.
  • One person in view. Nobody walks behind.
  • Measure height in shoes (cm) once per session.
Hub pose IDSevery action starts and ends in it · hold ≥ 1 s

IDS-R (Idle Dribble Stance, right hand):

  • Feet ~1.2× shoulder width, left foot ~15 cm ahead.
  • Knees bent 30–40°, hips back, chest over the knees.
  • Ball dribbled at knee-to-hip height beside and slightly ahead of the right foot.
  • Left forearm up as a guard.
  • Phase 0 = ball in the right palm at the top of the bounce.
  • IDS-L is the software mirror. Never record it.
  • Other connection states: JOG-R (right foot down, ball at the top) and LAND (both feet planted, no ball).
  • Hold ≥ 1 s perfectly still in IDS at the start and end of every action clip (≈ 2 bounces (est.)).
Loops20–30 s to a metronome · whole cycles
  • Record 20–30 s takes to a metronome. Idle ≈ 120 bpm (1 bounce per beat). Jog cadence 160–170 steps/min with a bounce every 2nd step (est.).
  • For lane loops, be at speed before entering the frame: start 3 m before the edge (sprint: accelerate over 8 m). Keep a constant pace. 2 passes each way.
  • Analyse a window with at least one whole cycle: idle 4 bounces ≈ 2 s, jog 2 strides ≈ 1.4 s. The builder finds the loop itself.
  • Speed tiers (est.): walk 1.4 · jog 3.0 · run 4.3 · sprint 5.8 m/s.
Hands & mirroringrecord right-handed · direction clips both ways
  • The ball always starts in the right hand. The game mirrors the 3D motion to get the left hand.
  • Mirroring flips hand and direction, so record these both ways, right-handed: strafe left + right, turn 90° L + R, cut 90° L + R, 180° plant on the left + on the right.
  • Record hand changes once, R→L (the mirror gives L→R): crossover, between the legs, behind the back, spin.
  • For lane loops, do both passes (L→R and R→L). Far-side joints are 40–108 % less accurate.
Turns & cutsturn through the camera side · V-path for cuts
  • Plant the pivot foot on the centre mark. Pivot radius ≤ 1 m.
  • Turn through the side facing the camera. Back to the camera for ≤ 0.5 s (a spin ≤ 0.3 s).
  • 90° cuts use a V-path: approach at 45° going away from the camera, plant on the centre mark, exit at 45° coming back.
Naming<family>-<action>[-<variant>]-<hands>-t<NN>
<family>-<action>[-<variant>]-<hands>-t<NN>
  • Families: calib dribble loco start stop turn cut move shot layup
  • Hands: r, or rl / lr for a hand change.
  • Lowercase, a-z 0-9 _ -, ≤ 60 characters.
  • Examples: dribble-idle-r-t02 · loco-jog-fwd-r-t03 · move-crossover-idle-rl-t03 · shot-stepback-r-t04 · calib-gym1-t01
The name only guesses the role, from its family prefix (move-crossover-… → crossover, loco-run-… → forward loop, shot-… → jumper, dribble-idle-… → idle hub). Always pick the role in /mocap → 3D game clip and Save.
Calibration~12 s, once per camera position
  1. Clap or slam the ball (an audio sync mark).
  2. A-pose facing the camera on the centre mark, 2 s.
  3. Quarter turn to profile, 2 s.
  4. IDS-R, 2 s.
  5. Slow walk along the whole lane, stepping flat on the tape marks.
  6. 2 small hops, then stand still 1 s.

Write down height in shoes (cm), tape distance (m), camera height and distance (m). Put them in the clip notes. The pipeline does not read calibration clips automatically yet.

Takes & order≥ 3 good takes per action · shots before fatigue
  • ≥ 3 good takes per action (3–5 for moves and shots). 2 passes per direction for lane loops.
  • Session order: calibration → idle hub → locomotion → transitions → shots → moves.
  • Say the take name on camera, or rename the file straight after recording.

Analyse settings in /mocap

The Clip type buttons on /mocap's Capture step set these. Then trim to the action plus its holds. Max frames goes up to 240 (8 s at 30 fps). Measuring costs about $0.02–0.03 per frame; the 3D body mesh adds ~$0.02 per frame.

Why 30 fps: a jog step touches the floor ~0.25–0.3 s, which is only ~3 samples at 12 fps.

Import, step by step

  1. /mocap → Capture: choose the video and name it with the convention. Press the clip-type button, trim to the action plus its holds, and press Analyze motion.
  2. Motion step: press Add 3D body mesh + hand close-ups.
  3. 3D game clip panel (Motion step):
    • Choose the role.
    • Check the type, the trims (raw frames cut from each end; leave 0 for loops) and the entry max (actions; blank = auto).
    • Press Save & rebuild and read the quality flags.
    • Set rejected takes to — none —.
  4. Open in 3D court, or go to /court3d, to play it.

Troubleshooting: quality flags

Shown after Save & rebuild in /mocap. Red = bad (the clip will not work well), amber = warning.
